# Containment

The following containment actions were documented:

- Disabled the compromised employee account.
- Isolated the affected workstation from the network.
- Blocked external IP addresses `41.2.3.10` and `185.9.2.1` at the perimeter.
- Reviewed activity on affected internal systems during the compromise window.

## Objective

Containment was intended to prevent continued use of the compromised credentials and limit further communication or movement from the affected workstation.
